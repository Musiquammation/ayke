Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ayke collects, uses, stores, and protects user information when you use the Ayke game and its related services.

1. Account Creation

Creating an account is optional. You can play Ayke without creating an account.

If you choose to create an account, we may collect and store information necessary to create and manage your account, such as your username, authentication information, and game progression data.

2. Game Data

Ayke may collect and store gameplay-related information, such as your scores, trophies, progression, selected game settings, and other information necessary to provide the game's features.

3. Public Leaderboard

Ayke provides a public leaderboard. If you create an account and participate in the leaderboard, certain game-related information associated with your account may be publicly visible to other users.

This may include your public username and your number of trophies or other leaderboard-related statistics.

The leaderboard is intended to allow players to compare their game progress and achievements with other players.

4. Information We Do Not Require

Creating an account is not required to play Ayke. We do not require you to provide personal information such as your real name, postal address, or phone number in order to play the game.

5. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and operate Ayke and its online features.
  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Save game progression and trophies.
  • Provide the public leaderboard.
  • Maintain the security and reliability of our services.
  • Detect and prevent abuse or fraudulent activity.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information associated with your public leaderboard profile may be displayed to other Ayke users as described above. We may also disclose information when required by applicable law or when necessary to protect our services, users, or legal rights.

7. Data Retention

We retain account and gameplay information for as long as necessary to provide the relevant services or until the account is deleted, unless we are required to retain certain information for legal or legitimate security purposes.

9. Children's Privacy

10. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
